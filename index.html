Got Questions? We've Got Answers.

How do I get a Rudra Telecom connection? ▼

Simply share your name, phone number, and location through our connection form. Our team will check availability and get in touch with you.

How do I know which plan is right for me? ▼

It depends on how you use your connection. Tell us the number of users, your typical usage, and whether it's for home or business. We'll help you choose a suitable plan.

Is Rudra Telecom available in my area? ▼

Enter your location or PIN code in the connection form, and our team can check availability in your area.

How long does installation take? ▼

Installation time depends on your location and the availability of the required infrastructure. Our team will confirm the expected timeline when processing your request.

Can I upgrade my plan later? ▼

Yes, depending on the available plans and your location. Contact our team and we'll help you choose an upgrade.

Do you provide business connections? ▼

Yes. We offer connectivity solutions for businesses with different requirements, including options for growing teams and enterprise users.

What should I do if I face a connection issue? ▼

Contact our support team with your registered details and we'll help you troubleshoot the issue and get you back online.
